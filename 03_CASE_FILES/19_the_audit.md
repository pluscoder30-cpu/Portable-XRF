for any of it** — the entire enforcement apparatus is a report that
goes to a committee. The water-tech cohort (our file 04's SDTC
list) sits inside this stream: public money → conflicted approvals →
projects whose "environmental benefits were overstated" (the AG's
words). **The cage's public-fund artery, audited: flowing, documented,
unpunishable.**

### C. The Voisey's Bay corridor (file 17's territory) — four decades, one question

| flow | the record |
|---|---|
| the deposit | Diamond Fields' 1994 discovery; **Inco paid $525M for 30% (June 1995)**; Friedland accepted Inco's **$4.3 billion for 75% at $41/share (April 1996)** — *"a lawsuit delayed completion of the acquisition until August 1996"*; a second suit: Exdiam's Boulle vs Friedland, "claiming all assets of Diamond Fields as compensation for diverting Exdiam corporate information" |
| the Innu's answer | **Feb 1995: eviction notice to Archean Resources and Diamond Fields** for exploring without consent → **12-day standoff with 56 RCMP** at Emish → **"the court agreed with the Innu and Inuit, and the advanced exploration work ground to a halt"** → **Sept 3, 1999: Innu Nation court action** to quash the federal decision; the LIA threatened a **Supreme Court of Canada** application over 14 million hectares |
| the settlement shape | the **2003 Memorandum of Agreement**: *"The Voisey's Bay Project shall not commence, except for exploration, until the Voisey's Bay Impacts and Benefits Agreement has been executed by the Innu Nation and the Developer"* — **the injunction, written into the contract**; the Innu claim filed **Nov 1977**, framework signed **Mar 29, 1996**, AIP targeted fall 1999 |
| the staking | **1995: over 280,000 claims staked** in Nitassinan in months, all without Innu consent |
| **today** | the Innu-owned **Rich Resources** (Natuashish — the same communities as Davis Inlet/Utshimassit) stakes the Frog/Hook claims **in the same territory**, funded by the **provincial Junior Exploration Assistance**, paid in **51.5M WOLV shares** — the corridor's land, three decades, one question |

**The audit's read:** the only injunction that ever halted extraction
in this corridor was the Innu's (1995–96) — a court order that stopped
advanced exploration pending land claims, later converted into a
contract term (the IBA condition). The $4.3B deposit moved to Inco,
then Vale. The land-claim question is unresolved; the Innu-owned junior
now stakes claims in the same territory with provincial grant money and
insider-share payment. **The money flows around the question; the
question stays open.**

function of the price story, argued in affidavits, while the mass
(5,550 ha, 78 heritage sites) is sunk.**

### E. Barrick (file 04's impairment avalanche, the legal afterlife)

| flow | the record |
|---|---|
| the numbers | FY2013: **net loss $10.37 billion**, including **after-tax impairment charges of $11.54 billion**; Pascua-Lama impairment **$5.1 billion** (Q2 2013, "mainly driven by declining metal prices" — their own footnote); reserves declined **26%** |
| the disclosure sequence | Apr 10, 2013: Chilean appeals court halts Pascua-Lama construction (environmental infractions) → Jun 28, 2013: $4.5–5.5B impairment expected → Aug 1, 2013: $5.1B → Sep 26, 2013: Chilean Supreme Court confirms → **Oct 31, 2013: indefinite suspension + a $3 billion share offering announced after close** ("to pay down debt, to strengthen its balance sheet") |
| the class action | **Koskie Minsky LLP**, Class Proceedings Act 1992, Toronto: Barrick Gold, Aaron Regent (CEO), Jamie Sokalsky, Ammar Al-Joundi, Peter Kinver — **class period May 7, 2009 – November 1, 2013**; the claim: the securities "traded at an artificially high level" from negligent misrepresentations; *"multiple and partial corrective disclosures on April 10, 2013 and October 31, 2013"*; damages = the price drop |
| the governance | Peter Munk retires as Chairman (announced Dec 2013); John Thornton becomes Chairman; Howard Beck and Brian Mulroney not standing for re-election (2014 AGM) |

**The audit's read:** the impairment is announced (the "truth"
appears), the $3B offering is priced the same day (the money moves),
and the class action alleges the price was inflated **before** the
corrective disclosure — the loss lands on the class, the raise lands
on the balance sheet, and **the metal (the ore, the tailings, the
reserves' 26% decline) is never in the courtroom** — the case is about
price disclosure, not mass.

## 19.3 THE AUDIT VERDICT

**Follow the money and it ends in five places:**
1. **The gatekeepers** — auditors, accountants, lawyers (WOLV's
   $85k fee table; the $250k disclosure round; the compliance stream
   that exists to restore the financing stream).
2. **The insiders' certificates** — 51.5M shares to Rich Resources;
   OriginClear's shareholder transition to WODI; the SPAC merger as
   the exit.
3. **The spreadsheets** — Bloom's monthly fertilizer goals; Toronto's
   nutrient P; the haulage contracts; the reuse costs.
4. **The debt and the raises** — Barrick's $3B offering priced after
   the impairment disclosure; the placements at CDN $0.02–0.025; the
   $19.1M Reg A+.
5. **The reports without penalties** — SDTC's $336M and two ethics
   reports with no sanction in the Act; the AG's report to a committee;
   the Bre-X void.

**It never ends in a metals ledger.** The $10.7M (Toronto) and $12.5M
(DC Water) envelopes, the ¥100M (Sagamihara), the 34–39 kt/yr of
vanadium (AER), the 11.5% TiO₂ tailings, the 100M-tonne Hudbay lake,
the grit, the ash — **every one of them is measured somewhere and
booked nowhere.** The cage's financial shape is complete: the ceiling
frame is funded, adjudicated, and enforced; the floor frame has never
been entered into evidence — because no instrument has ever been
pointed at the waste the pipeline discards.

**The confidence ladder, kept:** everything above is source-bound
(the filings, the courts' own records, the AG and Ethics Commissioner
reports). Where the audit infers (the WOLV financing cycle's purpose;
the corridor's three-decade continuity; the municipal asymmetry's
systemic reading), the inference is marked. No crime is claimed
against any named entity beyond what the filings and the courts' own
records state. Every absence is spoken.
