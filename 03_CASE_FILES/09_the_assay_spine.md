## 9.1 THE STRUCTURE (the user's hypothesis, confirmed)

```
the extractor (Suncor/Syncrude/Hudbay/Teck/Barrick/Alamos/Kore)
      ↓ SUBMITS samples
the assay chain (Bureau Veritas / SGS / ALS / Inspectorate / AGAT / Syncrude Research Lab)
      ↓ certifies grades
the resource estimator (SRK / GRE / global-resource-engineering QPs)
      ↓ declares reserves & resources
the accounting node (price-driven impairments;2012–13 avalanche)
      ↓ re-prices the declared value
the waste streams (tailings / ash / sewage / furnace-floor / dredge)
      → NEVER submitted to any lab in the chain
```

**The labs are not the cage.** They do exactly what they are paid to
do: assay the SUBMITTED sample, ISO 17025, round-robined, umpired.
**The cage is the SUBMISSION LAYER** — and the XRF is the first
instrument that can be pointed at the WASTE the pipeline ignores.
