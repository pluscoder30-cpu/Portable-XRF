## 12.2 THE SPLIT (what the P&L does not carry)

The FY2021 KES segment table has three columns: **Waste Treatment
Related, Water Treatment Related, Chemical/Food Machinery.** There is
no fourth column. The ¥100 million of gold recovered at Sagamihara
in the same fiscal year does not appear in any segment line, any
ordinary-income calculation, or any order backlog — because the
"resource recovery from urban mining" activity exists only as a
"spotlighted" item in a corporate-IR presentation, not as a revenue
line.

**The read (the cage's shape, by their own numbers):** the company
that BUILT the furnace recovered ore-grade gold inside it, disclosed
the recovery to investors in a presentation, and did NOT book it as
revenue — because the accounting frame (waste treatment) has no slot
for it. The scarcity narrative at the consumer end ("waste") and the
abundance evidence at the operator end (15 kg Au, 6,000 g/t sand)
coexist in the same fiscal year of the same company's filings, in two
different documents, unconnected. The XRF closes that gap: the reading
of the material is the column the P&L never had.
