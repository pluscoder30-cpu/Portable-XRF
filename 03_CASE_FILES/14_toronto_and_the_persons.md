## 14.2 THE TORONTO ARITHMETIC — two frames, one table

**Frame 1 — the ceiling (what the filing does):** the metals are
compared against regulatory MAXIMA. Cu runs at 40% of its ceiling, Zn
at 16%, Cr at 2.4%, Cd at 2%. The verdict: "met all criteria." The
frame values the metals only as potential CONTAMINANTS — "is it safe
to put on a field."

**Frame 2 — the floor (what nobody does):** the same numbers priced
at 2024 market rates:

| metal | annual avg (g/t dry) | × 38,341 dry t | contained | ~2024 price | ~value/yr |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cu | 672 | 25,765,152 g | **25.77 t** | ~$9,300/t | ~$240k |
| Zn | 670 | 25,688,470 g | **25.69 t** | ~$2,700/t | ~$69k |
| Mn | 281 | 10,773,821 g | **10.77 t** | ~$1,300/t | ~$14k |
| Cr | 68 | 2,607,188 g | 2.61 t | ~$8,000/t | ~$21k |
| Ni | 26 | 996,866 g | 1.00 t | ~$16,500/t | ~$16.5k |
| Pb | 32 | 1,226,912 g | 1.23 t | ~$2,100/t | ~$2.6k |
| Mo | 10 | 383,410 g | 0.38 t | ~$22/kg | ~$8.4k |
| Se | 4 | 153,364 g | 0.15 t | ~$50/kg | ~$7.7k |
| Cd+As+Hg | ~3.5 | ~134,000 g | 0.13 t | mixed | ~$0.8k |
| **measured metals subtotal** | | | | | **~$380k/yr** |
| Total P (agronomic, already booked via land application) | 31,896 | 1,222,881 kg | 1,223 t P | ~$1,000/t | ~$1.2M (booked as nutrient) |

**The gap:** the ASU/USGS standard values this stream at **$280/ton
(13 lucrative elements) → 38,341 t × $280 = $10.7M/yr** — the same
national standard, applied to Toronto's own tonnage. Toronto's
Appendix F measures **6 of the 13** lucrative elements (Cu, Mn, Zn, Cr,
Cd, P). The other seven — **Au, Ag, Pd, Ir, Ti, Ga, Al (and Fe)** —
are **never measured** in the biosolids analysis, because they are not
regulated. At the standard's own unit value, the unmeasured remainder
of the stream ≈ **$10.3M/yr** — a number that appears in no Toronto
document.

**The read:** one table, two frames. The filing carries the ceiling
frame ("met all criteria"); the floor frame ("worth $10.7M") requires
multiplying the same published numbers by the same published tonnages
— and nobody in the chain has done it. The 43,418 tonnes that went to
Ontario farmland in 2024 carry the measured metals at 40%/16%/2.4% of
their regulatory ceilings into the soil; the unmeasured seven elements
traveled the same route, unpriced. **The XRF is the floor frame.**
