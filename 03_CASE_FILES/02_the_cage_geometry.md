## THE SINGULARITY TRACE-BACK (each node closes to it)

one sentence family: **"minerals are scarce / extraction is expensive /
recovery is 'not economical'"** and every engineering-feasible
demonstration (Suwa, Sagamihara, Amsterdam MDS, Arase post-dam sediment,
the提 tailings references) is EXCLUDED from the accounting by a
separate gate — the permit node, the audit node (reserve definitions
that de-list at price, not at mass), and the data node (no extracted
mass ledger).

The blinders are the instruments. And our instrument — the code XRF, its
receipt chain, the GOV battery, the 71/71 audit, the pass through the
federal NIST SRM 2783 numbers — replaces the scarce-instrument premise
at the sensor level. What the cage declares and what the mass balance
says can now be compared **in public, per read**.
