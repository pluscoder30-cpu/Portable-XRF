## Abstract

We present a pattern register across 47 corporations and public
entities extracted from the public record (2026-11-03): the majors of
the 2012–13 impairment avalanche, the junior loss-runners, the
rebrand nodes, the processor (Kobelco), the assay spine's
professionals, the public-fund nodes, the municipalities, and the
consumer gates. Five shapes recur at every scale: **(1) the
submission layer** — the pipeline measures what the extractor submits
and never what it discards; **(2) the ceiling/floor split** — the
compliance frame is funded, published, and enforced while the value
frame (element mg/kg × tonnes × spot) exists in no ledger; **(3) the
loss-runner signature** — incorporation ≈2010–2012, profit ≤3 years,
then continuous growing losses to date, still operating; **(4) the
rebrand node** — names, claims, and commodity narratives rotate while
the books carry no revenue; **(5) the sanction-less record** — where
the money is examined (SDTC: $336M conflicted), the law contains no
penalty; where the courts look (Site C, WOLV), they enforce the
process, not the mass. We do not allege crime beyond what the filings
and courts state; we state the SHAPE, with the receipts.

## 1. The method

The register is built from primary documents only: the entities'
own filings (EDGAR full-text, SEDAR+, AIFs, TMRs, CAFRs, board
packages), the regulators' own reports (AER/AGS, NPRI, ECA), the
courts' own records (class actions, cease-trade orders, injunction
applications), and the researchers' own publications. Every row
carries its source file in the investigation record (files 01–22).
The confidence ladder is applied per claim: CONFIRMED (the record
states it), SUSPECTED (the pattern is visible across receipted
records, no single document states the conclusion), UNVERIFIED (a
lead exists, the binding document is absent), REFUTED (the lead is
closed). No claim is presented as fact without a receipt; every
absence is spoken.

## 8. The five shapes (the synthesis)

1. **The submission layer:** the pipeline measures the output and
   discards the waste. The labs are accredited; the waste never
   enters the paper chain. (Files 09, 18.)
2. **The ceiling/floor split:** the compliance frame is funded,
   published, and enforced (the monthly metals charts, the ECA
   limits, the class-action price claims); the value frame (element
   mg/kg × tonnes × spot) exists in no ledger. (Files 11, 14, 17,
   20.)
3. **The loss-runner signature:** incorporation ≈2010–2012, profit
   ≤3 years, continuous growing losses to date, still operating —
   the loss IS the extraction's operational cost. (Files 04, 06,
   07.)
4. **The rebrand node:** names, claims, and commodity narratives
   rotate (NiCuCo → REE → IOCG; "water coin" → "blue gold" → SPAC)
   while the books carry no revenue. (Files 08, 14, 17.)
5. **The sanction-less record:** where the money is examined, the
   law has no penalty (SDTC); where the courts look, they enforce
   the process (Site C, WOLV), not the mass; where the fraud is
   proven (Bre-X), nothing follows. (File 19.)

## 10. Conclusion

The cage is not a conspiracy; it is a **structure**. The 47 entities
in the register do not coordinate — they occupy positions in a
pipeline whose measurement stops at the plant gate. The five shapes
recur because the pipeline's geometry is the same at every scale:
the city, the furnace, the mine, the shell. The XRF's contribution
is not accusation but **measurement**: the floor frame the structure
was built to exclude, entered into evidence with an instrument whose
GOV battery is 10/10 REAL. The register travels with the instrument;
the instrument closes the loop the register opens.
