**Tagging discipline (the confidence ladder, kept):**
- **CONFIRMED** — the record is in the entity's own filings, the
  regulator's own reports, the court's own records, or the
  researcher's own publication. The fact is not in dispute.
- **SUSPECTED** — the pattern is visible across multiple receipted
  records, but no single document states the conclusion. The reading
  is of the shape, not a verdict.
- **UNVERIFIED** — a lead exists; the binding document has not been
  obtained. Named, not claimed.
- **NOT CLAIMED** — no criminal accusation against any named entity
  beyond what the filings and the courts' own records state. The
  tags are pattern-confidence, not legal verdicts.
